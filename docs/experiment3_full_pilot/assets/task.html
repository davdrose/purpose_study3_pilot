Welcome

Rellins and Daxons look very similar to one another. You will learn about these two unfamiliar known species and then categorize a newly discovered population that also looks very similar to them.

Your task
First, learn what the two known species are like. Next, gather two kinds of evidence about a new population. Finally, decide where that population belongs between the two species.
Instructions

The task will be introduced one part at a time.

Here is what will happen.

1
First, you will be introduced to a new location.
2
Next, you will meet two unfamiliar known species that live there.
3
Then, you will meet a newly discovered population from the same location.
4
Finally, you will scan a sample animal and decide where the population belongs.
Biological scan
The biological scan examines animals.
It reports three internal findings: genome, development, and body systems.
Purpose scan
The purpose scan examines animals.
It reports what the animals are for, how that benefits the animals, and the broader environmental benefit.

How you will use the scanners

Move the sample animal to the scanner shown on the screen.
You may drag the animal, or select it and then select the scanner. Both scan results will remain visible when you make your categorization judgment.
