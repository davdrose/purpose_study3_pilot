Shared prompt specification:

> Create one photorealistic [ANIMAL], full body visible, centered in a neutral side or three-quarter standing pose. Isolate the animal on a seamless pure white background with even studio lighting and no visible cast shadow. Natural anatomy, coloring, fur or skin texture, and proportions. No scenery, ground line, plants, props, other animals, people, text, border, or watermark. Scientific-stimulus clarity, consistent framing, generous white margin around the animal, landscape composition.

The animal name replaced `[ANIMAL]` for each separate generation.
